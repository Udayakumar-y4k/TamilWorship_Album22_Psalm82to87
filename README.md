# Album 22 – Psalm 82–87: The Justice & Glory of God
Album 22 – Psalm 82 to 87 Anointed Tamil worship songs proclaiming God’s justice, sovereignty, and glory. Freely given – All glory to Jesus Christ.

Psalms 82–87: God judges the unjust rulers (82), prays for the nations to know Him (83), longing for God’s presence (84), restoration and revival (85), cry for mercy (86), and glorious Zion (87). Points to the righteous reign of King Jesus.
All glory to Jesus Christ.
